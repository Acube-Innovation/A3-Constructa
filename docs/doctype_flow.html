Step 1 — Set up once

These are filled in at the start of a job and then only picked from. None of them is submitted: saving is enough.

DoctypeWhat it holdsWhat the system does for you
Cost Head The packages of work — University, Hospital, Road works — with their project and cost centre. Kept as a tree, so a package can sit inside a bigger one.
WBS The parts each package is built from — Substructure, Superstructure — under a project and cost head. Also a tree. The level (1, 2, 3…) is set automatically from where it sits.
Cost Code The kind of cost — concrete, rebar, plant hire. Category (Material, Service, Subcontract, Spare, Rental), plus the account and cost centre it posts to. Material issued against it posts to its account and cost centre. Whoever issues the material never has to pick an account.
Approval Matrix Who approves what: by doctype, project, item group, amount band (from / to) and level, with the approving role or user. A register of the approval rules. The BOQ sign-off itself runs through its own approval flow (next page).
Port & Service Route Sea, air and land ports; routes between two ports with transport mode, transit days and shipping line. Picked on shipments and freight contracts.
Freight Rate Contract The agreed freight rate from a supplier for a route and container type (20FT, 40FT…), with free days and valid-from / to dates. The rate to check freight charges against.
Document Type The kinds of paper a delivery carries — Certificate of Origin, CNF Invoice, Duty Payment Receipt, Delivery Order. Picked in a shipment’s document list.
Mandatory Document Rule The documents a shipment must have, optionally only for Import or Domestic. Tick Is Blocking to enforce it. A blocking rule stops a shipment being submitted until every listed document is in with its original received.
Asset Spare Part Which spare fits which machine or asset category, with part no., minimum stock, reorder qty, store and preferred supplier. Gives each machine its spares list.
Asset Category ERPNEXT One field added: a numbering series per category, e.g. HEQ-.#### for heavy equipment. New machines are numbered from their category: HEQ-0001, VEH-0001.
Get the three tags right first. Cost Head, WBS and Cost Code are what every later doctype points back to. A missing cost code on an issue line means that cost cannot be reported against the works.

Step 2 — Plan

BOQ-2026-0001 BOQCONSTRUCTASUBMITTED The bill of quantities: what the job needs, how much, and at what rate.
You enter Project, cost head, date and revision no. One line per item with its WBS, cost code, BOQ qty and rate — then the approved qty and approved rate.
System works out Amount = qty × rate on every line, and the total. Budget amount = approved qty × approved rate. The budget reports use this figure.

Approved means submitted and locked. The Project Manager can still adjust it while it is pending.
To revise Cancel the approved BOQ (its status becomes Rejected) and amend it. The copy goes through approval again.
WBSA-2026-0001 WBS AllocationCONSTRUCTA Splits the approved BOQ across the parts of the works, so each WBS has its own budget.
You enter Project, BOQ, WBS and cost head. Lines with item, cost code, allocated qty and rate. Each line points back to the BOQ line it comes from.
System works out Allocated amount = qty × rate. Balance qty = what is left of that BOQ line after every allocation of it. A negative balance means the line is over-allocated.
PP-2026-0001 Procurement PlanCONSTRUCTA Works back from the date material is needed on site to the date it must be requested.
You enter Project and cost head. Lines with item, WBS, cost code, BOQ qty, anticipated qty and required on site date. Lead time is copied from the item.
System works out Recommended PR date = required-on-site date − lead time. A line with no required date gets no recommendation. Status (Draft, Submitted, Completed, Cancelled) is set by hand.

Step 3 — Buy

Standard ERPNext doctypes. Constructa adds the fields below; the buying steps themselves work as ERPNext normally does.

DoctypeConstructa addsWhy it matters
Material Request Cost Head, WBS, Cost Code on every line; an Is MAS Item flag. The site’s request is tied to the works from the first step.
Supplier Quotation Technical evaluation status and remarks; Is Recommended with remarks. The chosen quote is marked, and shows in the Recommended Quotations report.
Purchase Order Cost Head, WBS, Cost Code on every line. From here the money counts as committed in Budget vs Commitment.

Step 4 — Deliver

SHP-2026-0001 Shipment TrackingCONSTRUCTASUBMITTED One record that follows a delivery from the supplier to the store or site.
You enter Project, supplier, purchase order, Domestic or Import, direct to warehouse or site. Then, as it moves: ports and B/L, departure and arrival dates, customs (bill of entry, duty paid), container return, demurrage and detention, inland transporter and LR no. Plus two tables:
  • Milestones — FAT Cleared → Vessel Sailed → Customs Cleared → … → Received at Site, each with planned and actual date.
  • Documents — each paper, its number, and whether the original has been received.
On save Transit days = arrival at port − departure from origin. Days late per milestone = actual − planned date. Refuses an arrival before its departure, inland or at sea.
On submit Checks the blocking Mandatory Document Rules. If a required document is missing, or its original is not ticked as received, submit is refused and the missing documents are named.
Good to know Keep it as a draft while the shipment moves and submit when it is complete. After submit only the status can change.
DoctypeConstructa addsWhy it matters
Purchase Receipt Cost Head, WBS, Cost Code on every line; Is Site Receipt (SMRN), site receipt no. and receiving site. Records what arrived. Goods taken straight to site are flagged as an SMRN.
Landed Cost Voucher Duty % and duty account on each Customs Tariff Number. Freight, duty and clearing go onto the item, so stock carries its true cost.

Step 5 — Use & pay

MIN-2026-00001 · MRN-2026-00001 Stock EntryERPNEXTSUBMITTED Every stock movement. The type at the top says which kind it is.
Types Material Issue Note (MIN) · Material Receipt Note (MRN) · Site Material Return · Tool Issue · Tool Return · Tool Write-off
You enter Items, qty and stores, with Cost Head, WBS and Cost Code on each line. Transport details on the header: mode, transporter, vehicle no., LR no.
On save An MIN is numbered MIN- and an MRN MRN-. Every line with a cost code takes that cost code’s account and cost centre, replacing the default. To post a line elsewhere, clear its cost code.
On submit Stock moves. For an MIN, this is the moment the material becomes a cost to the job.
TIS-2026-0001 Tool IssueCONSTRUCTASUBMITTED Hands tools to a person. Tools are stock held in that person’s custody store, not fixed assets.
You enter Date, project, site, from store, to the person’s custody store, issued to (employee), expected return. Lines: tool, serial no., qty, condition on issue.
On save Records each tool’s value at issue, so a tool lost later is recovered at that value. Status is set from the lines: Issued, Partially Returned or Returned. Lost, Damaged and Written Off are set by hand and kept.
On submit Creates and submits a Stock Entry (type Tool Issue) that moves the tools into the custody store, dated the issue date. Cancel reverses that entry too.
Coming back A Stock Entry of type Tool Return moves it back to the store; a lost tool goes out through Tool Write-off.
WC-2026-0001 Work CertificateCONSTRUCTASUBMITTED Subcontractor work measured for a period, with retention held back.
You enter Project, cost head, WBS, subcontract PO, supplier, certificate no. and period. Lines: item, cost code, contracted qty, previous qty, this period’s qty, rate, retention %.
On save Amount = this period’s qty × rate  →  Retention = amount × retention %  →  Net payable = amount − retention, with totals of all three. Refuses a line where previous + this period is more than the contracted qty.

Step 5, continued — people, plant and the end of the line

DoctypeConstructa adds / does
Asset Make, model, serial, engine and chassis no., capacity, warranty dates and project. Numbered from its category (HEQ-0001).
Timesheet Each line tagged to a WBS, cost code and machine, so hours are costed to the works.
Attendance Project and site, so site labour can be spread across the jobs they worked on.
Expense Claim Cost Head, WBS and Cost Code on each line.
Serial No Its item group is filled in automatically, which is what makes the small-tools register work.
